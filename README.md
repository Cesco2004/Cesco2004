## Featured project

### [Are prediction markets efficient? A pre-registered research programme](https://github.com/Cesco2004/prediction-markets-research)

Independent research, October 2025 – May 2026. Five studies across horse racing, tennis,
football, Kalshi and Polymarket.

- **Question**: do betting exchanges and prediction markets price outcomes correctly, or show
  persistent, exploitable biases?
- **Data**: 4.4 M Kalshi trades, Polymarket and Pinnacle prices, 40,000+ football matches,
  ATP match data.
- **Method**: every confirmatory test is pre-registered before the run. Strict hold-out,
  Bonferroni control (13 strategies tested, 1 survives), adversarial internal reviews.
- **Result**: a venue-specific reverse favourite-longshot bias on Kalshi passed a strict hold-out
  (+18.2 % net yield, 95 % CI [+6.2, +30.7], N = 892). Live paper trading was inconclusive.
  Four of the five studies are documented nulls or falsifications.
- **Papers**: two working papers on the Kalshi bias, in
  [`papers/`](https://github.com/Cesco2004/prediction-markets-research/tree/main/papers).
